Install on a real iPhone (2 minutes)

1. Once the app is on the cloud server (next step), open its address in Safari on the iPhone.
2. Tap the Share button (square with ↑).
3. Tap “Add to Home Screen” → Add.
4. The UK Transit icon appears — opens full-screen, no browser bars, Dynamic-Island-aware, just like a native app.
Until the cloud step, this works on your PC only (the phone can't reach localhost). After the Oracle deployment it works anywhere — that's also what "runs with the laptop closed" gives you.
